Model vibes

A personal tool that, once a week, collects public posts about AI models from X, Reddit and Hacker News, sorts what people say each model is good and bad at, and emails the summary to its owner, Jordan Moshcovitis, and to recipients he has explicitly approved.

It uses Gmail only to send that weekly email from the owner's account to the owner and those approved recipients. It does not read mail. Only the owner signs in to the tool; recipients just receive the email.
