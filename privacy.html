Privacy policy

Model vibes is a personal tool run by Jordan Moshcovitis (jordan.moshcovitis@gmail.com) for his own use.

Google account access

The tool asks for the gmail.send permission plus your email address, only to send its weekly report from the owner's Gmail account to the owner. It cannot read, search or delete mail. The access token is stored only on the owner's computer and is never shared.

Data

The tool processes public posts from X, Reddit and Hacker News. It does not collect data about visitors to this page and has no other users. The weekly reports are not published.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
