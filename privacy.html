Privacy policy

Model vibes is a personal tool run by Jordan Moshcovitis (jordan.moshcovitis@gmail.com).

Google account access

The tool asks for the gmail.send permission plus your email address, only to send its weekly report from the owner's Gmail account to the owner and to recipients he has explicitly approved. It cannot read, search or delete mail. Only the owner signs in; recipients do not grant the tool any access. The access token is stored only on the owner's computer and is never shared.

Data

The tool processes public posts from X, Reddit and Hacker News. It does not collect data about visitors to this page. The weekly reports are emailed to the owner and approved recipients only and are not published. Recipients' addresses are kept on the owner's computer and used only to send the report; a recipient can ask to stop receiving it at any time.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
